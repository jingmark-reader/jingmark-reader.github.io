Privacy

Your documents stay on your device

Local-first and upload-free: we only touch folders you explicitly authorize, sanitize every render with DOMPurify, bundle third-party libraries locally, and ask for the minimum permissions.

No uploads

Nothing is sent to a server; all processing stays on your machine.

Authorized folders only

We read and write only folders you allow, and you can revoke access anytime.

Sanitized & strict CSP

Rendering and export are double-sanitized; only local scripts ever run.

Libraries bundled locally

marked, Vditor, and docx ship inside the extension — no remote loads.
