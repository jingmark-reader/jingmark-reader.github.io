Refunds & orders

All purchases are processed by our payment partner Creem. JingMark never stores your card details.

  • Pro is a one-time purchase — there is no recurring subscription to cancel.
  • For a refund or to manage your order, use the Creem customer portal link sent to your email after purchase, or simply email us and we'll help.
  • Refunds follow Creem's refund policy and applicable consumer law.

Common questions

  • Where are my files? JingMark reads and writes only folders you explicitly authorize via the File System Access API. Nothing is uploaded to any server.
  • How do I activate Pro? Click “Upgrade to Pro” in the extension, sign in with your email, and complete the Creem checkout. Your status syncs automatically across devices.
  • Which browsers are supported? Any Chromium-based browser with File System Access API support (Chrome 86+, Edge). Firefox support is coming soon.
  • Forgotten password? Use the reset link on the login page.
